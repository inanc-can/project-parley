# AgenticPay

**Paper:** *AgenticPay: A Multi-Agent LLM Negotiation System for Buyer-Seller Transactions* (arXiv 2602.06008) — `paper.pdf`

Code: `code/` (git submodule, pinned commit; `git submodule update --init` to fetch).

## What it is
Buyer–seller negotiation benchmark with private constraints and product-dependent valuations, from bilateral bargaining to many-to-many markets (110+ tasks). Extracts structured actions from language and reports feasibility, efficiency and welfare metrics.

## Useful for Parley
- Task variety for buy/sell templates (multi-product, markets).
- Its feasibility / efficiency / welfare metrics as a cross-check for Parley's surplus metrics.

## Caveats
- Commerce-focused; less on social dilemmas.
- A recent (2026) preprint: check whether there is a peer-reviewed version before citing.
