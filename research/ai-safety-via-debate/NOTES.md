# AI Safety via Debate

**Paper:** Irving, Christiano & Amodei, 2018 — *AI safety via debate* (arXiv 1805.00899) — `paper.pdf`

## What it is
Two agents debate a question in turns and a human judge picks the more truthful one. The proposal argues that, in the optimal play of this zero-sum game, agents tell the truth, so debate can help supervise systems more capable than their judges.

## Useful for Parley
- The judge-reads-the-transcript setup mirrors Parley's judge LLM, so it can inform judge reliability studies.
- Debate is a zero-sum conversational game Parley can express (judge-only scoring).

## Caveats
- No code; paper only.
